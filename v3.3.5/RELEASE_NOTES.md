# Radar 1.28 v3.3.5

Released: October 11, 2026

## What's New

- Added interactive maps to radar and weather web settings, including click-to-select, panning, zoom controls, and radar coverage preview.
- Added OpenStreetMap Standard, Germany, and France tile sources with automatic availability selection.
- Manual coordinate entry remains available when map tiles cannot be reached.
- Weather can follow the radar coordinates or keep an independent location.
- Redesigned the aircraft information card with a two-column icon layout and fixed-wing/rotorcraft differentiation.
- Callsign colors continue to match aircraft targets on the radar.
- Improved 100 km stability during extended operation and simultaneous web access.
