# Radar 1.28 v3.3.1

Released: October 2, 2026

## What's New

- Added bilingual aircraft information cards with clearer compact text.
- Callsign colors now match the corresponding aircraft target.
- Double-click enters or exits persistent aircraft information mode; single-click cycles aircraft while the card is open.
- ADS-B polling is deferred while an aircraft card is open for smoother interaction.
- Improved 100 km low-memory rendering and secure-connection cleanup.
- Fixed low-memory restarts during continuous aircraft-data updates.
- Filtered test callsigns from the radar display.
